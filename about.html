Devoted Hearts Protecting Every Animal Life

Founded with pure compassion, Stackly Animal Sanctuary rescues abandoned strays, performs life-saving veterinary surgeries, and builds lifelong adoption bonds across communities worldwide.

◆ Working together for a better world.

Every action has a long lasting impact on an animal's life.

We are a trusted animal welfare and emergency rescue sanctuary dedicated to defending vulnerable strays and wildlife across our communities. With a coalition of licensed veterinarians, trauma specialists, and compassionate volunteers, we deliver end-to-end rehabilitation services including round-the-clock emergency medical dispatch, critical surgeries, nutritional nurseries, and ethical adoption placements.

Our goal is to simplify animal advocacy and create sustainable sanctuary infrastructure where every neglected, injured, or abandoned animal is rehabilitated with dignity, warm shelter, and lifelong loving companionship.
